## Required submission

- Project name, short description, final team name and officially registered members.
- Public GitHub repository; include instructions for judges to reproduce/build the app.
- Working demo video of one minute or less.
- Post the video on X or LinkedIn and submit the post URL.
- Tag Cognition/Devin and include the AppBuildersPH hashtag. Copy exact handles/hashtag from the form rather than trusting auto-captions.
- Paragraph disclosures covering models, frameworks, APIs/cloud services, existing code/assets, and AI development tools; explain what runs locally and what requires internet.
- Explain why running AI locally benefits this product.

Submission is through the [Cerebral Valley event page](https://cerebralvalley.ai/e/appbuildersph-hackathon-2026). README documentation complements the form; it does not replace it. No submission has been made by this checklist.

## Rules and deadlines

- Substantial work during the hackathon, meaningful local inference and a working product required. Open-source reuse and AI-assisted development are allowed, with disclosure (14:21–16:26).
- Only officially listed participants; no outside human assistance. Fake benchmarks can be disputed (18:25–19:27; 33:49–34:05).
- One project/submission per team; no edits or resubmissions (34:37–35:25; 37:23–37:31).
- Deadline and code freeze: 10 AM October 10, no extensions or commits after the deadline (37:34–38:20).
- Missing public repo, demo video or social-post URL can cause screening rejection (25:03–25:30).
- Live deployment/installable package is not mandatory; reproducible repo instructions required. A deployed link is a bonus. TestFlight is not necessary for submission (41:49–42:11; 46:55–47:09).

## Finalist presentation

- Five-minute pitch including live demo, followed by three-minute Q&A (21:02–21:35).
- At least one representative must attend in person; absent finalists may lose their slot (38:35–39:28).
- A prerecorded video cannot substitute for the finalist live demonstration (45:45–45:57).

Judging from instruction photos: usefulness 25%, local AI 25%, execution 20%, innovation 15%, product/demo quality 15%.
